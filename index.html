Hypotheses under test (H1 to H5)

  • H1 Large faces with relationship tags and phone endings reduce wrong-contact picks.
  • H2 Hold-to-pay prevents accidental sends, or it is harder for shaky hands than a big Tap button.
  • H3 Reading the amount and name aloud helps users catch mistakes.
  • H4 A short send delay with a big Cancel button saves mistaken payments.
  • H5 Larger text and no icon-only buttons shorten time on task.

Honest constraint: Real UPI payments cannot be recalled once sent to the bank or NPCI. The 8-second “sending soon” delay in Saral is a design concept where the payment app holds the instruction briefly on the phone before transmitting it.
